Ways to play

01 / CUSTOM ROOM

Custom rooms

Create a room and share its code or invitation link. Up to four players can play together, with additional seats for spectators.

Ranked matches

Use matchmaking to compete for seasonal standings. RTG changes depend on results and your opponent's rating.

04 / KNOCKOUT

Knockout tournaments

Follow a tournament bracket and advance round by round. This is a separate format from Ranked rating-based matchmaking.

02 / MATCH FORMAT

One round or three?

ModeRoundsResult
Ranked1The round's result decides the match.
Regular / AI3Settled using the complete match's rounds and accumulated results.
Knockout3Advancement has additional tiebreaking rules.

A single-round match uses the same placement and closure-scoring rules. The difference is when the match result is decided.

03 / RATING

Ranked & RTG

Ranked uses an Elo rating system. A new account starts at 1200 RTG. The first 10 matches are a placement period, during which rating changes may be larger. Ratings continue to adjust afterward based on both players' ratings and match results.

TierRating thresholdAdditional requirement
BronzeBelow 1100Also used provisionally during placement
Silver1100+Complete placement
Gold1200+Complete placement
Platinum1300+Complete placement
Diamond1450+Complete placement
Master1600+At least 30 completed matches
Placement status: Placement is a rating status, not an extra official tier. Tiers, badges and seasonal displays may change; refer to the current in-game season information.
