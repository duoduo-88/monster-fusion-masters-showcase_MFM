01 / GAME RULES

Game rules

Monster Fusion Masters is a turn-based strategy game. Connect hexagonal tiles and close white regions to score. These are the basic rules currently used in the online edition.

Reference: Competitive Rule / Rule 20200629 · Online rules checked: 2026-10-16.

01 / BOARD

The hex board

The board contains 61 hexagonal spaces: a center surrounded by four rings. Each space holds at most one tile. You cannot place a tile outside the board or on an occupied space.

Placed tiles affect the legal orientations and connections of neighboring spaces. Each move changes the combinations available for future turns.

02 / TILES

Tiles & the deck

Each tile has six edges, each either white or black. There are seven categories, from 0 to 6 white edges. Tiles in the same category may have different edge arrangements and internal connections.

Deck composition: The number of 1-white-edge tiles is twice the number of each of the 2-white-edge and 3-white-edge categories. These three counts vary with the deck selected for the round. Each player may hold at most 5 tiles.
03 / PLACEMENT

Making a legal move

  1. Select a tile from your hand and rotate it to the desired orientation.
  2. Choose an empty space within the board.
  3. Every edge touching an existing tile must match: white to white, black to black.
  4. If any touching edge does not match, choose another orientation or space.

Use the placement preview to check legal moves. You can also rearrange your hand without changing the tiles themselves.

04 / CONNECTIONS

Connections, closure & DEAD

Matching white edges form one or more white regions according to the internal connections of each tile. Adjacent tiles do not necessarily join the same region: their white edges must also connect inside the tiles.

A white region with no open exits becomes a closed shape that can score. A region that has already scored cannot earn points again through repeated checks.

A white region extending beyond the board boundary has no space to continue and is marked DEAD. This is different from successful closure and does not count as a closed scoring region.

06 / MATCH FLOW

Turns, passing & matches

Each round starts with an initial hand. On a player's turn, tiles are drawn according to the available deck and hand limit. The player may place a tile or choose PASS.

3 consecutive passes eliminate the player from further participation in that round. Points already earned in the round are retained. A successful placement resets the consecutive-pass count according to the game flow.

Regular matches use 3 rounds; Ranked matches use 1 round. The final result follows the actual match format and settlement rules.

Turn timers: Time limits depend on the mode. A timeout may trigger an automatic PASS. The current online game is the authority for official rulings.
