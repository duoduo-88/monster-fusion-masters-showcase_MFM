ABOUT / MFM

About MFM

I'm DUODUO88, a hobbyist independent game developer.

Monster Fusion Masters (MFM) is an original strategy board game that I designed. It began as a physical tabletop game and gradually evolved into a free online multiplayer competitive game.

My goal is to create meaningful strategic depth through simple, accessible rules, with an emphasis on spatial planning, risk management, and player interaction.

MFM is a personal passion project that I continue to develop and improve in my spare time.

Thank you to everyone who plays the game, shares feedback, and helps make it better.
